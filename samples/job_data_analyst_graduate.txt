Graduate Data Analyst

We are looking for a curious Graduate Data Analyst to join our Insights team on our 2026 graduate programme.

Responsibilities
- Clean, analyse and interpret large datasets to support business decisions
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards and automated reports for stakeholders
- Write SQL queries to extract data from our data warehouse
- Use Python or R for statistical analysis and forecasting
- Present insights clearly to non-technical stakeholders

Requirements
- A 2:1 degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics or a related quantitative subject
- Strong SQL and Excel skills
- Experience with Python (pandas) or R
- Excellent communication and problem solving skills
- Strong attention to detail and the ability to manage time across several projects

Desirable
- Experience with Azure or AWS would be an advantage
- Knowledge of machine learning is a plus
